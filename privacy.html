Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The Coleman Aristocracy Uploader ("the App") is an internal tool used only by The Coleman Aristocracy to manage its own YouTube channels.

What the App accesses

Through YouTube API Services, the App accesses only the YouTube channels owned by The Coleman Aristocracy that their owner has authorized. It uses this access to upload and schedule videos, update video and channel details, post and reply to comments on those channels, and read channel and video statistics.

Data we store

The App stores, in the owner's private Google Drive: authorization tokens for the owner's channels; upload logs; video statistics; and public comment information on the owner's channels (commenter display name, channel ID, comment text and our replies) so replies can stay consistent. We do not collect data from anyone other than through public comments left on our own channels.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Daily performance summaries are emailed only to the channel owner and his team.

Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our channels you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The channel owner can revoke the App's access at any time at Google security settings.

Retention and deletion

Stored data is kept only as long as needed to run the channels. To ask us to delete comment information about you, email morningstartantraatl@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.
